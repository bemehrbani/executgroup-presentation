The current digital portfolio encompasses three distinct delivery models, each with different user interaction patterns.

MANAGED SERVICE No Password

Aura Suite

WorkWell, Lead, Continuity, Customer

Access: Direct link via email token
FREE TOOL Self-Serve

VS (VSME) Tool

Voluntary Sustainability Reporting

Small business users register with email and password to prepare, store, and export voluntary CSRD/VSME reports.

SAAS PLATFORM Subscription

DMA (ESGClick)

Double Materiality Assessment

Corporate sustainability leads and consultants log in with enterprise accounts to manage DMA stakeholder campaigns.

Access: Enterprise SaaS Login

Path Comparison

Comparing the Two Primary Paths

A side-by-side examination of how user journeys, branding, and sign-in behave in each model.

Path A: Dedicated Domains TWO HUBS

ExecutGroup + ExecutESG

Separate web properties tailored to specific buyer personas and intent.

ExecutGroup.com Focuses on leadership, wellbeing, continuity, and advisory. Navigation drives to consultation ("Book a discussion"). No top-level login button needed.
ExecutESG.com Dedicated to CSRD, VS reporting, and DMA software. Retains standard "Sign In" button for active reporting users.
Strengths: Targeted buyer messaging; no login confusion; preserves existing ESG search equity.
Trade-offs: Two web properties to maintain; requires cross-site navigation links.
Path B: Unified Domain SINGLE HUB

ExecutGroup.com Sole Brand

A single corporate platform housing all advisory services and software tools.

ExecutGroup.com Encompasses all offerings. Includes a top-level "Sign In" button opening a multi-destination selector (VS Tool, DMA SaaS, or Survey link).
ExecutESG.com 301 redirected to ExecutGroup.com sections (e.g. /sustainability).
Strengths: Concentrates all marketing traffic and domain authority on one brand; single site to maintain.
Trade-offs: Requires multi-door login modal; 301 redirects across all indexed ESG pages.
Available Domain Assets: ExecutGroup.com (corporate), ExecutESG.com (sustainability), and AuraOS.fi (internal/technical). Model Comparison
