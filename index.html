Technical & Commercial Reality

The Three Portals & The Sign-In Dilemma

Our users belong to three completely distinct access archetypes. Putting a generic "Sign in" button on the ExecutGroup corporate homepage causes immediate confusion.

TURNKEY SERVICE No Password

Aura Suite

WorkWell, Lead, Continuity, Customer

Participants evaluate pairs using private one-time email tokens. No registration or credentials required.

Token URL: /participate/{uuid}
FREE TOOL Self-Serve

VS (VSME) Tool

Voluntary Sustainability Reporting

Small business users register with email and password to prepare, store, and export voluntary CSRD/VSME reports.

Standard Auth: Email + Password
SAAS PLATFORM Subscription

DMA (ESGClick)

Double Materiality Assessment

Corporate sustainability leads and consultants log in with enterprise accounts to manage DMA stakeholder campaigns.

Enterprise SaaS Login
⚠️ Key Insight: Enterprise executives exploring Aura diagnostic services should NOT encounter a confusing multi-login gate. Their primary next step is talking with an advisor.

Recommended Blueprint

Deep Dive: The Dual-Hub Strategy

Separating properties by audience and buying intent eliminates friction while preserving every euro of existing software recurring revenue and SEO equity.

ExecutGroup.com CORPORATE & ADVISORY

Executive Diagnostics & Decision-Making

Dedicated to leadership alignment, workplace wellbeing, customer insight, and business continuity.

  • ✓ Products: AuraWorkWell, AuraLead, AuraCustomer, AuraContinuity
  • ✓ Delivery: High-value turnkey advisory & diagnostics
  • ✓ Primary CTA: "Contact us" / "Discuss your situation"
  • ● NO "Sign In" button (eliminates buyer confusion)
Cross-link: "Looking for our free sustainability tool? Visit ExecutESG →"
ExecutESG.com SUSTAINABILITY HUB

Compliance Software & DMA

Dedicated to CSRD, ESRS compliance, voluntary SME sustainability reporting, and Double Materiality software.

  • ✓ Products: VS (VSME) Tool & DMA SaaS Platform
  • ✓ Delivery: Self-serve freemium software + SaaS subscription
  • ● Prominent "Sign In" button for active reporting users
  • ✓ Retains 100% of organic Google search traffic
Cross-link: "An ExecutGroup Company | Strategic Diagnostics →"
Note on AuraOS.fi: Preserved as internal API backbone or for future Finnish municipal/public tenders. Hub-and-Spoke Synergy
