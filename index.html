Technical Landscape

The Three Portals & Access Archetypes

The current digital portfolio encompasses three distinct delivery models, each with different user interaction patterns.

MANAGED SERVICE No Password

Aura Suite

WorkWell, Lead, Continuity, Customer

Participants evaluate pairs using private one-time email tokens. No registration or credentials required.

Access: Direct link via email token
FREE TOOL Self-Serve

VS (VSME) Tool

Voluntary Sustainability Reporting

Small business users register with email and password to prepare, store, and export voluntary CSRD/VSME reports.

Access: Standard Email + Password
SAAS PLATFORM Subscription

DMA (ESGClick)

Double Materiality Assessment

Corporate sustainability leads and consultants log in with enterprise accounts to manage DMA stakeholder campaigns.

Access: Enterprise SaaS Login
ℹ️ The Design Trade-Off: A top-level "Sign In" button is essential for self-serve software users, but is unused by token-based survey participants and executive advisory prospects. Designing the navigation requires balancing these two needs.

Technical Recommendation (Mahdi & Veini)

Our Recommendation: The Dual-Hub Strategy

"From these options, we recommend Route A (Dedicated Domains), because..."

1

Buyer Mindset & Conversion

C-suite and HR leaders evaluating leadership or wellbeing diagnostics expect consultative engagement. Placing a generic SaaS login on their landing page creates cognitive friction.

Result: Pure consultative CTAs
2

Zero SEO & Revenue Risk

ExecutESG.com already has Google search equity and inbound links for CSRD, ESRS, and VSME. Preserving it eliminates the risk of keyword drops and preserves software ARR.

Result: 100% traffic & rank retention
3

Frictionless Participation

Aura survey participants evaluate pairs via one-time private email tokens. They never need a password or login page. Active software users log in seamlessly on ExecutESG.

Result: Zero authentication confusion
Alternative Option: If leadership prefers a single brand, Route B (Unified Domain) with a multi-door login modal is fully supported technically. Technical Brief

Company Name Change: Practical Impact Playbook

A structured checklist to execute the transition across emails, LinkedIn, PRH, DNS, and customer touchpoints.

1

Microsoft 365 & Emails

  • • Add executgroup.com to current M365 tenant (zero migration needed).
  • • Configure dual aliases: receive mail at both domains in one inbox.
  • • Set up SPF, DKIM, and DMARC DNS records for 100% deliverability.
  • • Standardize team email signatures with updated branding and phones.
2

LinkedIn & Social Channels

  • • Rename existing LinkedIn Page (preserves 100% of followers & history).
  • • Update custom URL to linkedin.com/company/executgroup.
  • • Sync team profiles: employees update current company experience.
  • • Publish coordinated founder announcement post outlining the expansion.
3

Legal, PRH & Invoicing

Domains, Search & Systems
